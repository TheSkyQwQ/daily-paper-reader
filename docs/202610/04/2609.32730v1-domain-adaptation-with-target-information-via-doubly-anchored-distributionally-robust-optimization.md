## 摘要
域适应（DA）往往缺乏最坏情况保证，而仅基于源数据的分布鲁棒优化（DRO）将其模糊集中心设在源分布律处，并忽略了可用的目标结构。为弥合这一差距，我们提出一种双重锚定 DRO 框架，其模糊集是以源分布律和源补全目标参考分布律为中心的 φ-散度球的交集；后者将目标协变量分布律与源条件分布律配对。我们针对对称与非对称散度配对推导出对偶诱导的对抗桥几何，尤其引入了 Kullback--Leibler/平方 Hellinger（KL/HD）桥。这种非对称形式产生一种 Lambert-W 几何：源侧指数风险倾斜和目标侧 Hellinger 稳定化通过不同项进入，从而减弱但不限制大似然比的影响。此外，在不施加协变量偏移假设的情况下，我们在有界重叠条件下，为一个结构性的、与损失无关的密度桥风险的最小化者建立了有限样本泛化界；这些界并不直接适用于损失感知的 DRO 极小极大估计量。我们将该框架转化为桥加权的 Nadaraya--Watson 估计量，证明了源回归函数的一致相合性和逐点渐近正态性，并在源与目标回归函数重合时（如在协变量偏移下）实现目标恢复。最后，在域偏移的 Fashion-MNIST 数据集上的实证评估表明，在严重的合成目标协变量污染下，非对称 KL/HD 桥具有有限样本稳定性。

## Abstract
Domain Adaptation (DA) often lacks worst-case guarantees, while Distributionally Robust Optimization (DRO) based only on source data centers its ambiguity set at the source law and ignores available target structure. To bridge this gap, we introduce a doubly-anchored DRO framework whose ambiguity set is the intersection of $φ$-divergence balls centered at the source law and a source-completed target reference law, the latter pairing the target covariate law with the source conditional law. We derive dual-induced adversarial bridge geometries for symmetric and asymmetric divergence pairings, notably introducing a Kullback--Leibler/squared-Hellinger (KL/HD) bridge. This asymmetric formulation yields a Lambert-$W$ geometry in which source-side exponential risk tilting and target-side Hellinger stabilization enter through distinct terms, attenuating, but not bounding, the effect of large likelihood ratios. Furthermore, without imposing covariate shift, we establish finite-sample generalization bounds for the minimizer of a structural, loss-agnostic density-bridge risk under bounded-overlap conditions; these bounds do not apply directly to the loss-aware DRO min--max estimator. We translate our framework into a bridge-weighted Nadaraya--Watson estimator, proving uniform consistency for the source regression function and pointwise asymptotic normality, with target recovery when the source and target regression functions coincide, as under covariate shift. Finally, an empirical evaluation on a domain-shifted Fashion-MNIST dataset illustrates the finite-sample stability of the asymmetric KL/HD bridge under severe synthetic target-covariate corruption.
